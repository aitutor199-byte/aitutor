# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction

AI Tutor ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our educational platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- Name and email address
- Education level and preferences
- Learning progress and statistics
- Subscription and payment information

### 2.2 Usage Data
- AI question requests and responses
- Learning activity and study time
- Quiz and examination results
- Image uploads for question solving
- Voice session transcripts

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
- Device information
- IP address
- Application usage patterns
- Performance data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- Provide and improve our educational services
- Generate personalized learning recommendations
- Track learning progress and achievements
- Process subscriptions and payments
- Provide customer support
- Analyze usage patterns to improve the platform
- Send notifications and updates (with your consent)

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:
- Service providers (e.g., Supabase for hosting, Gemini for AI processing)
- Legal authorities when required by law
- Business partners (with your explicit consent)

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Row Level Security (RLS) in our database
- Secure API key management
- Regular security audits

## 6. Your Rights

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Export your data
- Manage notification preferences

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is designed for students. We:
- Collect minimal personal information from children
- Require parental consent for users under 13
- Implement age-appropriate safeguards
- Never knowingly collect data from children under 13 without consent

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as needed to provide our services, unless you request deletion. Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.

## 10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: support@aitutor.com
